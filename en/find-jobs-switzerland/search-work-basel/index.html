More about this search for cross-border applicants

Basel currently lists 178 job openings, making it the third-largest employment market in Switzerland after Zurich and Geneva. The city's economy is anchored by pharmaceuticals, with Roche and Novartis headquartered here, alongside a strong presence in life sciences research, logistics, and financial services. Cross-border workers from northern Italy, particularly from Como, Varese, and the province of Sondrio, form a significant share of the workforce, drawn by competitive salaries adjusted to Swiss living costs and the strong Swiss franc. Commuting is straightforward via the A2 motorway and regional rail connections through Chiasso. Employment permits are tied to the bilateral agreements between Switzerland and the EU, with quotas applied for non-EU nationals. Most roles require German language proficiency, particularly in administrative and customer-facing positions.
