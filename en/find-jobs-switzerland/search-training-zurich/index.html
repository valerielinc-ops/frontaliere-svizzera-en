More about this search for cross-border applicants

Zürich is Switzerland's largest employment hub, and its training jobs reflect the breadth of the regional economy. The 247 listings currently aggregated on this page include roles in corporate learning and development, vocational apprenticeship coordination, technical and IT training, healthcare education, and fitness and sports instruction. Major employers are typically finance, insurance, pharmaceutical, and technology companies headquartered in the city, alongside hospitals, universities, and professional training institutes. Candidates should expect contract types ranging from permanent positions in human resources to project-based instructor roles. For cross-border workers commuting from northern Italy, the role of training specialist or apprenticeship coordinator is often based in central Zürich, which means they are generally not suitable for daily commuting from Ticino. Remote or hybrid arrangements, however, are increasingly common.
