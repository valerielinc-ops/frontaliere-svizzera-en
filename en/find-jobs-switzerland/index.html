G permit and border zone

The G cross-border permit is reserved for residents of a border municipality in a country neighbouring Switzerland who return home daily: for Italian residents that mainly means Ticino, Graubünden and Valais. For roles in cantons farther from the Italian border — like the ones listed here — the common path is a B residence permit or relocating to Switzerland.
