More about this search for cross-border applicants

Zurich is Switzerland's largest economic centre and home to the country's highest concentration of multinational headquarters, banks and corporate service providers. Business roles across the canton cover areas such as accounting, controlling, consulting, human resources, sales and executive management. Cross-border workers, often referred to as frontalieri, frequently commute from northern Italy into Zurich and the wider German-speaking region, drawn by competitive gross salaries, the absence of a national wealth tax and strong demand for qualified professionals. Current listings on the platform include positions in finance, operations and strategy, with vacancies distributed across the city of Zurich and surrounding municipalities. Candidates should review work permit requirements, as conditions differ from the B permit issued in Ticino, and confirm applicable cross-border tax arrangements before applying.
