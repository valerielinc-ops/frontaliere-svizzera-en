More about this search for cross-border applicants

Bern, the federal capital of Switzerland, hosts a broad and resilient labour market shaped by public administration, healthcare, education, financial services, and a growing technology cluster. The 195 work opportunities currently aggregated for the city span entry-level positions, skilled trades, and professional roles across both the German-speaking core and surrounding communes. Bern's unemployment rate tends to sit below the national average, reflecting steady demand from cantonal employers, university-linked research institutions, and SMEs along the Aare. Candidates should note that Bern is outside the Italian-speaking region, so cross-border commuters based in Lombardy or Piedmont would typically rely on standard Swiss work permits rather than the frontalieri regime applied in Ticino. Salary levels are negotiated in Swiss francs and generally align with federal collective agreements where applicable.
